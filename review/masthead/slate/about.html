About the imprint

The frame, not the picture.

Division 26 is a small independent publisher of first-person true crime. We handle the business so our authors can carry the story.

The name comes from the courthouse. Division 26 was a room where cases were decided, sometimes fairly and sometimes not. We took it as a promise: publish the accounts of the people who stood in rooms like that and can tell you what really happened.

We are deliberately quiet as a brand. The imprint is a steady, neutral frame, and the color on any given page belongs to the author who earned it. Gregg Owen's is red. The next writer will have their own.

We publish in print, in audio, and on screen, and we keep the list short so every title gets real attention.
