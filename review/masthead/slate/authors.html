Gregg Owen

Prosecutor and author · Signature color, red

Gregg Owen spent years as a felony prosecutor in Chicago, trying sixty five jury cases inside a courthouse that ran on cash. His debut, Convergence, follows a double murder, a wealthy defendant, and a system for sale.

He now writes and speaks about the cases he lived, and hosts the Case Files series.
