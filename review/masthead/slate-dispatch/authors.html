Jonathan Dixon

[Author · Signature color, teal for now]

Jonathan Dixon was raised in New Hampshire and has lived in New York City since 1992. He started his writing career covering underground music for the Boston Phoenix and documented the culinary arts program at the Culinary Institute of America in the book Beaten, Seared, and Sauced (Random House/2011). His first collaboration with former prosecutor Gregg Owen, Convergence (Division 26), was published in 2025. Their second collaboration, Pursuing Kings, is due out in early 2027.

Praise for Jonathan Dixon’s Beaten, Seared, and Sauced

Beaten, Seared, and Sauced, Jonathan Dixon’s account of his chef-training at the CIA, is funny, gripping and immensely enjoyable. It reads like a picaresque novel.The Wall Street Journal
How lucky for those of us who are fascinated by food and the people who make it that Jonathan Dixon chose to go to the CIA and to write about it. All about it. With wit and insight and a hefty dose of humor. You could probably learn just a smidgen more if you went to the CIA yourself, but it wouldn’t be nearly as much fun as sitting in your favorite chair, sipping your favorite drink, and reading Jonathan’s story.Dorie Greenspan, author of Around My French Table

Gregg Owen

[Prosecutor and author · Signature color, red]

Gregg Owen was the keyboardist for the bands Shady Daze, the American Breed, Jerico, and Chill Factor. He served as an Assistant State’s Attorney and Cook County Chief of Gang Prosecutions in the city of Chicago. A frequent podcast guest, he is a content creator, recording studio consultant, and the founder of Text2Drive. Convergence is his first book.
