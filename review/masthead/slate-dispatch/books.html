Convergence

Jonathan Dixon • Gregg Owen · 2025

About Convergence

Neighbors heard screams coming from the apartment at the end of the hall, but no one called the police. Two days later, a young actress is found stabbed to death on the living room floor. Her boyfriend’s body is in the bathroom a few feet away. The Area 6 homicide detectives make an arrest within the week. The case is cleared. The suspect is arraigned, and then everything starts to unravel.

It was a crime with too much blood but no motive, committed by a murderer who was allowed to walk away.

The cops got no explanation why the two beautiful and privileged young people were murdered. The killer thought the family’s money meant he’d never have to give one.

Meanwhile, rock musician Gregg Owen passed the Illinois bar exam and gained local fame as a fierce and tenacious prosecutor. Years after the double murder, he got a call from his brother. The case had dissolved and was set to be dismissed. Owen and his partner, Mike Goggin, decided that even if they never found a motive, they had to bring the brutal facts in front of a jury.

Convergence tells the story of the extraordinary next steps. It involves paranoid witnesses, crooked judges, and disappeared evidence, millionaires and drug smugglers, bribe givers, bribe takers, porn, and politicians.

It’s about justice, collisions, coincidence, and luck. It takes Goggin and Owen across the country and back and brings together an unlikely mix of people tied to the crime.

Convergence opens the closed-door world of a murder prosecution wider than ever before. It’s a deep look into the strategy and courtroom choreography of two young and undefeated Cook County prosecutors, told in a sharp new voice with an insider’s perspective.

Pursuing Kings

Jonathan Dixon • Gregg Owen · 2027

Three people are senselessly murdered by members of the brutal Royal Family gang under a bridge. The timid man who drove the mastermind and witnessed the killings is terrified that he’ll be next. But the Royal Family just moves on to its next project: an interstate murder-for-hire.

Meanwhile, young musician-turned-prosecutor Gregg Owen suddenly finds himself in a position of great power and responsibility: Chief of Chicago’s brand-new Gang Crimes unit. Projecting absolute confidence under a constant media spotlight, Owen privately grapples with the complexities of his first case – from the ethical obligations owed to witnesses, to the moral implications of the death penalty, to the risks he must take to get gang bangers behind bars and keep his promise to protect communities.

The backdrop is Chicago at its grittiest and most corrupt, and Owen gradually realizes that many of those around him can’t be trusted. Meanwhile, Roger Collins, the leader of the Royal Family, wrestles with his impulsive tendencies—not because of the harm they inflict but because they’re bad for the business side of murder.
