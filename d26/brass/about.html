About Division 26

Why we exist

The best true crime comes from the people who were actually there.

Division 26 is an independent true crime imprint founded by writers and a former Chicago prosecutor. We publish first-hand accounts of the cases that shaped the people who worked them, and we hold those voices together under one name.

We build and run the platform. The authors keep the story. Each writer gets a proper home, their own page, and their own color, so a growing list still reads as one imprint.

We work across formats, print, audio, and screen, because a case worth telling deserves more than one way in.

An independent true crime imprint. Chicago and beyond.
